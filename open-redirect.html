Open Redirect

An open redirect happens when an app lets user input control where the browser goes next. A crafted link can start on a trusted site and send visitors to a different one.

  • OWASP A01:2025 Broken Access Control
  • CWE-601

Why it matters

A trusted domain in the first URL can make a phishing link look legitimate.

Real-world case · Credential phishing campaign (2021)

Microsoft documented a campaign that abused open redirects on legitimate sites. The links started on familiar domains and sent visitors through redirects to fake sign-in pages designed to collect credentials. This lab simulates the change of destination.
